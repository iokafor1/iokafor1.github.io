DAYMARK — PERSONAL FINANCE TRACKER

INSTALL ON IPHONE
1. Publish the contents of this folder to a static website with HTTPS. The app needs a secure website address for iPhone home-screen installation.
2. On your iPhone, open that HTTPS address in Safari.
3. Tap Share, choose “Add to Home Screen”, then tap Add.
4. Open Daymark from its new home-screen icon. After its first load, the app shell is cached for offline use.

The ZIP contains the app files for downloading or publishing. Opening index.html directly from Files is useful for a quick preview, but does not enable home-screen installation or offline caching.

DATA AND PRIVACY
Transactions and budgets are stored in the browser on that device. They are not synced to other devices or backed up online. The first launch includes example transactions; tap “Clear sample data” in the Recent transactions panel to remove them before entering your own records. Avoid using a shared device for private financial information.

The dashboard uses USD and starts with example monthly budgets. Edit the budgets inside the app to fit your plan.
